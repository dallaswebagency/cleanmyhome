What it is

Perfect for homeowners preparing for an open house or hosting guests, this light refresh provides a quick, high-impact polish to make your home look clean and inviting, without the time or expense of a standard or deep clean. It focuses exclusively on high-visibility, priority areas.

What it isn’t

Not a detailed or comprehensive cleaning. It does not include washing of baseboards or door frames, cabinet exteriors, inside appliances, detailed scrubbing, grout work, or moving items to clean underneath. For a thorough, top-to-bottom clean, our standard or deep cleaning packages are the ideal choice.
