Touch-Up Package

What it is

Perfect for homeowners preparing for an open house or hosting guests, this light refresh provides a quick, high-impact polish to make your home look clean and inviting, without the time or expense of a standard or deep clean. This service focuses exclusively on high-visibility, priority areas to give your space an immediate, pristine visual appeal.

What it isn’t

To keep this option fast and cost-effective, this service is not a detailed or comprehensive cleaning. It does not include washing of baseboards or door frames, cabinet exteriors, inside appliances, detailed scrubbing, grout work, or moving items to clean underneath. If your home requires a thorough, top-to-bottom detailed clean, our standard or deep cleaning packages are the ideal choice.
